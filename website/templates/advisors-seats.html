What the seat is

The workspace you use with your own clients

One seat is for one advisor. Your clients bring their holdings from any brokerage, with no brokerage login.

  • Clients who arrive ready

    A new client comes to the first meeting with their holdings, their goal and a fee check already entered.

  • Meeting prep and a proposal in about ten minutes

    What changed since the last review, talking points drafted for you to edit, and a proposed mix set beside today's.

  • Progress reports

    For last month, last quarter or the time since your last one. Your client reads it in their own account.

  • Notes that are never deleted

    Notes your client sees and private notes only you see. You archive instead of delete, and edits keep the earlier text.

  • Records you can download

    Each client's record - notes, proposals and reports - downloads as one file, to keep with your own books.

  • Your client's own view

    Each client has their own account: their goal, their plan, your notes and your name on it, between meetings too.

  • A log your client can see

    Each page you open in a client's account is listed for them: who, which page and when. Never their figures.

What the seat isn't

Software for the clients you already have

Not a listing or leads

A seat doesn't include a directory listing, leads or referrals, and no one is introduced to you. Northwend doesn't refer, rank or match advisors.

Not your planning software

Keep the planning software you use. Northwend is the front door and the view your clients open, which planning software usually doesn't have.

Unless you plan without one

For advice-only planners who never needed a full planning tool, the seat can be the whole workspace.

The agreement, in plain words

Four things we agree on

A paid seat comes with an advisor agreement. Its four main points are below; the Terms of Use and Privacy Policy apply too.

  1. You're the registered professional, and the advice is yours

    Proposals, notes, messages and reports carry your name and your firm's. Northwend doesn't review, approve or supervise them.

  2. Northwend is software and records only

    It isn't an adviser or a broker, it doesn't act for you or your clients, and it never recommends you or any investment. We don't sell investments.

  3. Clients own their data and keep it if they leave

    A client's account is theirs: they can download it, stop sharing it with you, and keep it if they stop working with you. Your notes and the records you sent stay with you.

  4. Our liability is limited to the fees you paid

    Northwend's total responsibility to you is limited to what you paid us in the 12 months before a claim.

Before you start

Common questions

Do I need my compliance team's approval?

That depends on your firm. Many firms ask advisors to get approval before using outside software, and advisors at larger firms almost always need it, so check with your compliance team first. Your own obligations, such as client disclosures and keeping records, stay with you and your firm.

How is my clients' data kept safe?

Every advisor account uses two-step sign-in with an authenticator app. Uploads aren't kept, account numbers are cut to the last 3 digits, and the AI sees holdings as percentages only. You see only the accounts your clients share with you. More in the Privacy Policy's security section.

Can I cancel?

Yes, any time, in one step in the app. You can still sign in and download your records afterward, and your clients keep their own accounts.

What do my clients see?

Their own account: their holdings, goal and plan, the notes, proposals and reports you share with them, your name and firm, and the list of pages you've opened in their account. They never see your private notes or anyone else's account.

Does Northwend hold client money or place trades?

No. It isn't a broker or a custodian and isn't connected to any brokerage. Money and trades stay with each client's brokerage.

Which brokerages do my clients need?

Any. Clients bring holdings in from their brokerage's own export, a paste, or by typing them in.
