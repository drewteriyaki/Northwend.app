How it works

Three steps, about a minute

  1. 1Copy

    Copy your plan's fund list

    From the enrolment page or the plan's website - one fund per line, in whatever shape it's shown. Headings and performance figures in between are fine.

  2. 2Paste

    Paste it into the decoder

    Optionally type what you put in each month, to see each yearly fee in dollars. Any balances in what you paste are left out.

  3. 3Read

    Read it line by line

    Each fund's kind, its yearly fee and where that fee comes from. When it can't be sure which fund a line is, it says so plainly instead of guessing.

What it is, and isn't

It describes. The choice stays yours.

The decoder explains what's on the menu. It doesn't rank the funds or say which to choose - your plan's materials, or a professional, can help with that. Fees come from public fund data and can change; your plan's fee disclosure has the ones that apply to you.

An educational tool, not financial advice.
