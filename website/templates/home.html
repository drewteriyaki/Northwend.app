Small and steady

Why people come back

Investing works best when you keep at it without watching every day's ups and downs. Northwend gives you a small reason to look in, and nothing to rush.

  • Every day

    Today's minute

    A quick question about how investing works, a myth or fact, or a "what would you do?" It counts the days you learned something, and a missed day never takes anything away.

  • Every week

    Scout's weekly card

    From Friday's close through the weekend, your week: what changed and a few headlines. Monday to Thursday, the week ahead: announced dividend and earnings dates, and market holidays.

  • Every month

    The monthly walk, and a practice challenge

    A few minutes to update your holdings and compare your mix with the target you chose. And a challenge with pretend money on real past prices, counted only on sticking to your own rule - no leaderboard.

  • Along the way

    Your wins

    Things you did that pay off for years, like lower fund fees, getting your whole employer match or adding money three months in a row. Only you see them.

Already investing

Every account, one calm view

Schwab, Fidelity, Robinhood, a workplace plan - bring any of them in. Northwend reads your brokerage's own files, a paste, or what you type, so there's nothing to connect.

  • All your accounts together

    Today's value and your mix across every brokerage, updated through the day. Tap a holding for its own page: its chart, your position and its news.

  • Your goal and your plan

    Whether you're on track, what a steady monthly amount could do, and how far your mix has drifted from your target.

  • Income and dividend dates

    The income your holdings are expected to pay this year, and the pay dates already announced - each saying where it came from. Nothing is guessed.

  • Fees, explained

    What each fund's yearly fee costs you in dollars. Paste your 401(k) plan's fund list, or a fund's fact sheet, and see it in plain words. Try the 401(k) decoder without an account.

  • News on what you own

    A few recent headlines about what you hold or watch, each linked to the source that wrote it. Northwend doesn't write them or say what to do about them.

  • Answers in plain language

    Ask Northwend about anything you see, using your own goal and mix - never your dollar amounts.

Privacy first

Less is kept than you share

You can try everything with an example portfolio, and delete all your holdings yourself at any time.

What's stored, and for how long

  • {{CHECK}}No brokerage login. Northwend never asks for it and never connects to your brokerage.
  • {{CHECK}}Files aren't kept. An upload is read, then deleted. Only your holdings are saved: symbols, shares, cost and value.
  • {{CHECK}}Account numbers cut short. Only the last 3 digits are ever saved.
  • {{CHECK}}Percentages are enough. Enter shares of a pretend total and keep real amounts to yourself.
  • {{CHECK}}The AI sees percentages. Your holdings reach Ask Northwend as percentages - never dollar amounts, share counts, account names or numbers.
  • {{CHECK}}No ads, no tracking. Your data isn't sold, rented or shared for advertising.

What's an index fund, and do I have one?
