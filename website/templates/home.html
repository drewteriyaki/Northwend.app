How it works

Three steps, at your own pace. Nothing is connected to your brokerage, and you can stop at any waypoint.

  1. Waypoint 1

    Bring your holdings

    Paste them from your brokerage's website, upload a CSV, read a screenshot or type them in. Any brokerage works, and percentages are enough.

  2. Waypoint 2

    Set a goal

    Answer a few questions about what you're investing for and when. Your plan shows where you stand and what a steady path could look like.

  3. Waypoint 3

    Follow the route

    Get started takes you one waypoint at a time. Ask Northwend explains anything along the way, from what a fund is to why your mix drifted.

Privacy first

Less is kept than you share. You can try everything with an example portfolio, and delete all your holdings yourself at any time.

What's stored, and for how long

  • {{CHECK}}No brokerage login. Northwend never asks for it and never connects to your brokerage.
  • {{CHECK}}Files aren't kept. An upload is read, then deleted. Only symbols, shares and cost are saved.
  • {{CHECK}}Account numbers cut short. Only the last 3 digits are ever saved.
  • {{CHECK}}Percentages are enough. Enter shares of a pretend total and keep real amounts to yourself.
  • {{CHECK}}The AI sees percentages. Never dollar amounts, share counts, account names or numbers.
  • {{CHECK}}No ads, no tracking. Your data isn't sold, rented or shared for advertising.
Example conversation

What's an index fund, and do I have one?

An index fund holds every company in a list, like the 500 largest in the US, so you own a small slice of all of them at once. Two of your holdings work this way; together they're 58% of your portfolio.
