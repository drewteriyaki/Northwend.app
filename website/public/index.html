Already investing

Every account, one calm view

Schwab, Fidelity, Robinhood, a workplace plan - bring any of them in. Northwend reads your brokerage's own files, so there's nothing to connect.

  • All your accounts together

    Today's value, what changed and your mix across every brokerage, updated through the day.

  • Your goal and your plan

    Whether you're on track, what a steady monthly amount could do, and how far your mix has drifted from your target.

  • Income ahead

    The dividends and interest your holdings are expected to pay over the next twelve months, month by month.

  • A fee check

    What each fund's yearly fee costs you in dollars, and what it could add up to over time.

  • Your activity

    Buys and sells worked out from each update, or your brokerage's own history if you upload it.

  • Answers in plain language

    Ask Northwend about anything you see, using your own goal and mix - never your dollar amounts.

Privacy first

Less is kept than you share

You can try everything with an example portfolio, and delete all your holdings yourself at any time.

What's stored, and for how long

  • No brokerage login. Northwend never asks for it and never connects to your brokerage.
  • Files aren't kept. An upload is read, then deleted. Only symbols, shares and cost are saved.
  • Account numbers cut short. Only the last 3 digits are ever saved.
  • Percentages are enough. Enter shares of a pretend total and keep real amounts to yourself.
  • The AI sees percentages. Never dollar amounts, share counts, account names or numbers.
  • No ads, no tracking. Your data isn't sold, rented or shared for advertising.
Example conversation

What's an index fund, and do I have one?

An index fund holds every company in a list, like the 500 largest in the US, so you own a small slice of all of them at once. Two of your holdings work this way; together they're 58% of your portfolio.

Ask Northwend

A guide, not a salesperson

Ask Northwend explains investing in plain language, using your own goal and mix. It points the way and offers hints; it never tells you what to buy.

  • We don't sell investments. No funds of our own, no commissions, no trading fees.
  • No one pays to be mentioned. No fund company or brokerage pays Northwend to appear in examples or answers.

Educational, not financial advice. AI answers can be wrong, so check anything important before acting on it.
