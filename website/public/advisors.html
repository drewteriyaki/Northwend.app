The tools

Everything around the advice, in one place

Northwend handles the gathering, the tracking and the paperwork, so your time goes to the conversation.

  • Client setup links

    A one-time link for each client to set their own password. You never share or see it.

  • Every client on one page

    Each client's value, today's change, alerts, and who's due a review, with any client a click away.

  • Proposals

    Draft a mix on a client's plan, compare it with today's, and share it. Your client accepts or replies from their own account.

  • Meeting prep

    What changed since the last review, and talking points drafted for you to edit and keep as a private note.

  • Progress reports

    Prepare a report for last month, last quarter or the time since your last one, and send it. Your client reads it in their account, and a plan PDF is there for meetings.

  • Notes and next steps

    Notes your client sees, private notes only you see, and next steps you can both follow. Archive instead of delete, edits keep the earlier text, and each client's record downloads as one file.

  • Model portfolios

    Save your own model mixes once and use them across clients.

  • A Monday email

    Your week in numbers: reviews due, proposals accepted, open next steps. Counts only - no client names or figures leave the app by email.

  • Your name on it

    Your name, firm, contact details and a message, shown to your clients on their own pages.

For your clients

A calm place your clients will actually open

Clients bring holdings from any brokerage - pasted, uploaded or typed in - with no brokerage login. They see their goal, their plan and your notes, and Ask Northwend explains what they see in plain language between meetings.

  • Any brokerage. Schwab, Fidelity, Robinhood and others, all treated the same.
  • Education, not advice. The guide explains; the recommendations stay yours.
  • Their data stays small. Uploads aren't kept, account numbers are cut to the last 3 digits, and the AI sees their holdings as percentages only.
  • Two-step sign-in for you. Every advisor account uses an authenticator app.

Getting access

From request to your first client

  1. Request access

    Create an account as an advisor, with your firm and your CRD or licence number.

  2. We check your licence

    Usually within two working days. It confirms you're registered - it isn't an endorsement. Until then, you can look around as an investor.

  3. Turn on two-step sign-in

    With any authenticator app, the first time you sign in.

  4. Invite a client

    Send a one-time setup link. Your client agrees to the disclosures, sets a password and brings their holdings in.

Before you start

Common questions

Does Northwend hold client money or place trades?

No. It isn't a broker or a custodian and isn't connected to any brokerage. Money and trades stay with each client's brokerage.

Who gives the advice?

You do. Northwend is an educational tool: it explains and organizes, and its guide never tells anyone what to buy. Your own obligations, such as keeping records, stay with you and your firm. Each client's record downloads as one file to keep with yours.

Which brokerages do my clients need?

Any. Clients bring holdings in from their brokerage's own export, a paste, or by typing them in.

What does it cost?

Northwend is free while it's in beta. If it ever charges for anything, it will say so first, and nothing is charged without your say-so.

Can I see other people's accounts?

No. You see only the accounts linked to you as your clients, and each client sees only their own.
