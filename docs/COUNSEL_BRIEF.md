---

## 1. What Northwend is

Northwend (northwend.app; the app at go.northwend.app) is a free web app that
helps people **new to investing** learn, set a goal and follow their own
investments. It is in an open beta in the United States.

- **People bring their own holdings** from any brokerage by pasting, uploading
  their brokerage's export, or typing them in. Northwend never connects to a
  brokerage, never asks for brokerage logins, holds no money and places no trades.
- **Learning first:** short lessons, "practice money" on past prices, a goal and a
  plan, a monthly check-in, a stress test, a 401(k) fund-menu decoder (which
  describes funds and fees; it never ranks or picks), and soon Lost & Found
  (finding old 401(k)s and unclaimed property through official government sources).
- **An AI guide ("Ask Northwend")** explains concepts and does arithmetic. It is
  instructed never to recommend buying, selling or holding a security or a mix,
  and a checker on every sentence it writes enforces that. It receives holdings
  only as percentages, never dollar amounts or account details.
- **Advisors** (registered professionals, licence checked on FINRA BrokerCheck or
  the SEC's IAPD before access, re-checked yearly) can work with clients they
  bring: notes, proposals, progress reports and messages. Every advisor artefact
  carries a line saying the advice is the advisor's, not Northwend's.

**Who runs it:** one founder, today as an individual. An LLC and insurance
(cyber and tech E&O) are planned before any money is charged.

## 2. How it makes money today

It doesn't. Nobody pays Northwend: not users, advisors, brokerages or fund
companies. No ads, no data sales, no commissions, no referral fees. Brokerage and
advisor lists are alphabetical and never ranked. The published Terms and Privacy
Policy say so.

## 3. Where I want to take it

**Direction (decided):** market Northwend as "new to investing? start here".
When someone is ready, they can optionally find a guide: an advisor they can
contact for, for example, a **one-time review for a flat fee**. Newer advisors who
are still building a client base are a named audience: they can be found by
people already learning on Northwend.

**Built but switched off until your review:**
1. **"Find a guide" directory:** advisors' own listings (name, firm,
   registration and CRD with the public regulator link, credentials, how they're
   paid, minimums, who they serve, states, virtual or in person, a short
   description, a scheduling link). Shown alphabetically within a few filters
   (state, meeting style, fee model, who they serve, minimum). No ranking, no
   featured slots, no reviews, nothing counted about who browses.
2. **Introductions:** a person sends a message and, if they choose, a figure-free
   outline (asset-class percentages, goal type, timeline, stage). The advisor can
   reply, share a scheduling link or decline.
3. **Two-step consent to full sharing:** only after an advisor answers, the person
   can share their full account in two steps; the exact words shown are recorded
   (append-only, kept 7 years), as is every page the advisor opens in the account.
   Either side can end it at any time.
4. **An advisor agreement** advisors accept before using the advisor tools
   (draft text enclosed).

## 5. Questions for you

**The individual side (live today)**
1. With the design above (education, calculators, the person's own facts; no
   recommendation tailored to their answers; AI rules and output checks), does
   Northwend stay outside "investment adviser" under the Advisers Act and state
   law, including the publisher exclusion? What would change your answer?
2. A setting we call L3 would show a beginner which "common starting point" fits
   their own timeline (still general, kinds of funds only). Is that acceptable, and
   with what wording?
3. Are the Terms of Use and Privacy Policy (enclosed, live since October 6)
   adequate for a US-only beta? Specifically: no governing-law clause or
   arbitration today, a USD 100 liability cap, state privacy laws (CCPA/CPRA and
   others) at our size, and breach-notification wording.

**The advisor side**
4. Is the advisor agreement (enclosed) enough to make clear the advice, the
   supervision, Form ADV delivery and record keeping are the advisor's? Anything
   missing (e.g. Regulation S-P service-provider terms)?
5. The licence check is manual (BrokerCheck / IAPD at approval, yearly re-check).
   Is that a reasonable standard? Should a lapsed registration also pause access to
   clients' accounts?

**The directory and introductions (gate L2)**
6. Does listing advisors and passing on introductions, **with no payment from
   advisors tied to introductions or clients**, make Northwend a "promoter" under
   the SEC Marketing Rule (206(4)-1), a solicitor under state rules, or anything
   requiring registration? Does that change once advisors pay a flat seat fee?
7. Please review the listing fields, the filters (including a possible "offers a
   one-time review" filter), the alphabetical-only ordering, the intro and two-step
   consent wording, and the standing line. Our Terms currently say Northwend "does
   not refer clients to advisors" - how should that be reworded?

**Monetization**
8. For each model in section 4: is it permissible, and what does it require
   (disclosures, contract terms, registration, state rules)? In particular, does
   charging advisors a flat seat fee change your answer to question 1 for the
   individual side?
9. What should be in place before the first paid seat (entity, insurance,
   contracts, sales-tax handling through Paddle, refund terms)?

## 6. What I've already done to reduce risk

- Education-only design; no tailored picks; named funds only in general material.
- AI: one gateway, written rules in every prompt, a sentence-by-sentence checker,
  holdings sent only as percentages; never sent email addresses or account details.
- Privacy: no brokerage logins, uploads not stored, account numbers cut to 3
  digits, two-step sign-in (required for advisors and admins) with its keys
  encrypted at rest, data export and deletion on the Account page, retention
  periods published and enforced by a nightly job.
- Records: append-only consent and advisor-access logs (7 years), an append-only
  admin action log (1 year).
- Hosting: Render (US) behind Cloudflare with security headers (A+ on
  securityheaders.com); database on Neon (US); an incident and breach plan.
