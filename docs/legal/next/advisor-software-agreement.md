This agreement covers a paid Northwend advisor seat. The [Terms of Use](/terms)
and [Privacy Policy](/privacy) apply too; where they differ about a paid seat,
this agreement wins. You accept it when you buy a seat.

## 1. What you're buying

A seat is software: the Northwend workspace you use with your own clients -
your client list, meeting prep, proposals, progress reports, notes, records and
the client's own view of their account. One seat is for one advisor.

A seat does **not** include a directory listing, introductions, leads or any
client Northwend finds for you. Northwend does not refer, recommend, match or
rank advisors. If a directory is offered later, it will be described separately
before anything about it applies to you.

## 2. You are the registered professional, and the advice is yours

- You are a representative of a firm registered with the SEC or a state
  securities regulator as an investment adviser (or of a broker-dealer, where
  that is permitted), and the firm's name and the CRD or licence number you give
  us are yours and true. You'll tell us straight away if your registration or
  your firm changes.
- **All advice you give through Northwend is yours,** given under your firm's
  supervision: proposals, model portfolios, notes, messages, reports and
  anything you send or show a client. Northwend doesn't review, approve or
  supervise it. What you send carries your name and firm and says so.
- Your obligations stay yours: your client agreements and disclosures (Form ADV
  or your firm's), suitability and fiduciary duties, your books and records,
  your clients' consent to use Northwend with you, and your firm's compliance
  policies, including any approval your firm needs before you use outside
  software. Northwend is not your system of record: export what you need to
  keep.
- We check your registration (FINRA BrokerCheck or the SEC's IAPD) when you
  join and about once a year. If it can't be confirmed, we may pause your
  access; you can still download your records.

## 3. Northwend is software and records only

Northwend provides tools and keeps records. It is not an investment adviser,
broker-dealer, planner or law firm, it doesn't act for you or your clients, and
it is not your agent or your client's. You won't present Northwend as an
adviser, or say or suggest that Northwend recommends you, your advice or any
investment.

## 4. Price and payment

- **Price.** The price for your seat is shown before you pay. Founding seats -

  as long as the seat continues without a break. <!-- standard price later:

- **One flat fee.** Never a fee per client, per introduction, per lead, by

- **Price changes.** At least 30 days' notice before a new price applies to

- **Cancelling.** At any time, in one step, in the app. The seat runs to the

## 5. Clients own their data, and keep it if they leave

- **A client's account belongs to the client.** Their holdings, plan, answers
  and settings are theirs. They can see their own account, download everything
  in it, stop sharing with you, and keep their account if they stop working with
  you.
- **Your records are yours.** Your notes, and the proposals and reports you
  sent, stay with your account so you can meet your record-keeping duties, even
  after a client stops sharing. You can download a client's record at any time.
- You can see a client's account only while they share it with you. Each page
  you open in it is recorded (who, which page, when - never figures), and the
  client can see that list. Records of consent and of visits are kept for 7
  years.
- We use your and your clients' data only to run Northwend, as the Privacy
  Policy says. We never sell it, never use it for advertising, and never use it
  to choose or suggest an advisor for anyone.

## 6. If your seat ends

If you cancel, a payment fails and isn't fixed, or the seat ends for another
reason:

- your account stays: you can still sign in, **read your records and download
  them** <!-- for how long: decide, e.g. 90 days, then the account becomes an
  ordinary free one -->;
- you can't add clients, send proposals, reports or messages, or use the paid
  tools until the seat is active again;
- your clients keep their own accounts and everything in them. We'll tell them,
  in plain words and without figures, that you're no longer using Northwend's
  advisor tools, so they aren't left wondering.

## 7. Using it properly

You'll use Northwend only for your own clients, with their permission, and
lawfully. You won't share your sign-in, add someone's information without their
permission, try to reach accounts or data that aren't yours, get around limits,
scrape or resell the service or its market data, or use it to train AI models.
Advisor accounts must use two-step sign-in.

## 8. Security and confidentiality

We protect your and your clients' data with reasonable measures (described on
the Privacy Policy and the security page for advisors) and use it only to run
Northwend. If we learn of a breach affecting your clients' information, we'll
tell you without unreasonable delay, so you can meet your own duties. You keep
your sign-in safe and tell us at once if you think it has been misused.

## 9. Limits on what we're responsible for

- Northwend is provided "as is". We work to keep it accurate and running, but
  don't promise it will be uninterrupted or error-free, or that prices, figures
  or AI drafts are correct. Market data comes from third parties and can be
  delayed or wrong. Check anything that matters before you rely on it with a
  client.
- **Our total liability** under this agreement is limited to the fees you paid
  us in the 12 months before the claim. We're not liable for indirect or
  consequential losses, lost profits, or any client's investment losses.
- You'll cover our reasonable costs of a claim against us that comes from your
  advice, your breaking this agreement or the law, or your relationship with
  your clients.

Some places don't allow some of these limits; nothing here takes away rights
that can't be given up by agreement.

- If this agreement changes in a way that matters, we'll tell you at least 30
  days before it applies; you can cancel before it does.
- You can end it at any time by cancelling your seat. We can end it with 30

  can't be confirmed. Section 6 then applies.
- If Northwend shuts down, we'll give at least 30 days' notice where we can and
  refund any period you've paid for and won't get.
