> The yardstick for all website and app copy - sections 1 and 2 of the owner's master brief (Oct 2026), verbatim.

## 1. The sentence every feature must fit inside

> Northwend is free for individuals and never gives individual recommendations;
> advisors pay a flat seat fee and are listed equally; any advice comes from a
> registered firm the person chose, never from Northwend.

If a feature cannot be described without breaking that sentence, it is out of scope.

## 2. Revised principles

1. **Free for individuals; paid only by advisor seats.** No subscriptions, ads,
   commissions, referral fees, per-lead fees, success fees or revenue shares. No
   brokerage, fund company or advisor pays to be ranked, featured or recommended.
2. **Education, never recommendations — by construction.** The individual side
   explains, compares, and does arithmetic on the person's own numbers. It never
   concludes what a specific person should hold, buy, sell or change. Content that
   is derived from a person's answers is **descriptive and general** ("for a
   twenty-year timeline, a common starting point is…"), never prescriptive ("a mix
   for you"). Projections are always labelled hypothetical. Named funds appear only
   in general education that is identical for everyone.
3. **Privacy first.** No brokerage login or aggregation. Uploads read and deleted.
   Account numbers reduced to last three digits. Emails never contain figures. No
   tracking, no ads. Models never see individual holdings or account details
   (figures policy in section 5).
4. **Everyone listed equally.** Brokerages alphabetical, unranked. Advisors
   alphabetical within the person's own filters, unranked, never "matched" or
   "recommended" by Northwend. The person chooses.
5. **Calm by default.** No urgency, hype or trading nudges. Gamification rewards
   learning and habits only.
6. **The advisor is the fiduciary; Northwend is the software.** Every advisor is a
   representative of their own registered firm, responsible for their own advice,
   their own client agreement, and their own compliance. Northwend provides tools,
   records and a listing, and says so plainly to every client.
7. **Clients own their data.** A client can leave an advisor in one step and keeps
   everything. Advisors keep only what their record-keeping duties require (their
   own notes and sent documents), never the client's live data.
